Earnings proxy methodology

The proxy uses the top 24 SPY holdings, combines duplicate share classes, and weights 60-day changes in future fiscal-year EPS estimates by SPY holding weights. Analyst revision breadth is also shown. This is a partial-index proxy, not the official FactSet S&P 500 bottom-up EPS series.

Interpretation and limitations

Thresholds and freshness limits are operating hypotheses, not validated trading rules. The proxy requires at least 30% SPY weight coverage and 10 companies. Proxy inputs expire after 10 days; fallback inputs expire after 14 days. The regime is undetermined when core inputs are invalid. EPS revision is 100 × (current − prior) / |prior|; zero prior EPS is excluded. This preserves the direction of changes in losses, but comparability with the provisional FactSet seasonal benchmark remains a separate research question.

Data sources and dates

Observation dates and collection dates are shown separately. Collection does not confirm when the provider last revised each estimate.
