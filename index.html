    Earnings proxy methodology

    The proxy uses the top 24 SPY holdings, combines duplicate share classes, and weights 60-day changes in future fiscal-year EPS estimates by SPY holding weights. Analyst revision breadth is also shown. This is a partial-index proxy, not the official FactSet S&P 500 bottom-up EPS series.

    Interpretation and limitations

    Thresholds and freshness limits are operating hypotheses, not validated trading rules. The proxy requires at least 30% SPY weight coverage and 10 companies. Proxy inputs expire after 10 days; fallback inputs expire after 14 days. The regime is undetermined when core inputs are invalid. EPS revision is 100 × (current − prior) / |prior|; zero prior EPS is excluded. Seasonal adjustment is disabled. Raw revisions and revision breadth are descriptive observations; earnings ratings and the combined regime are withheld pending validation.

    Data sources and dates

    Observation dates and collection dates are shown separately. Collection does not confirm when the provider last revised each estimate.

    How pillar ratings are determined

    These are the current operating rules, not statistically validated trading thresholds. Higher vulnerability or shock means greater concern, not a forecast of a market decline.

    PillarMeasureRating rules
    Valuation Vulnerability

    Equity yield gap = 100 / forward P/E − 10-year real Treasury yield, in percentage points.

    HIGH: gap < 2.5%p. ELEVATED: 2.5%p ≤ gap < 4.0%p. LOW: gap ≥ 4.0%p. A smaller gap means a thinner valuation cushion.

    Discount-Rate Shock

    Changes in the 10-year real yield over 30 and 90 calendar days; term-premium change over 30 days, anchored to its own observation date. 100 bp = 1%p.

    HIGH if any condition holds: real yield 1M ≥ +25 bp; real yield 3M ≥ +50 bp; eligible term premium 1M ≥ +15 bp.

    Otherwise, EASING if real yield 1M ≤ −25 bp AND 3M ≤ −40 bp. All other cases are MODERATE. HIGH takes precedence; stale or missing term premium is excluded.

    Earnings Confirmation

    Raw 60-day EPS revision, upward-revised company weight within covered holdings, and 30-day analyst breadth: (upward revisions − downward revisions) / (upward revisions + downward revisions).

    UNRATED: no HIGH / LOW or STRONG / WEAK thresholds are currently applied. Seasonal adjustment is disabled. Positive revisions or breadth indicate upward changes, not a validated earnings-strength rating.

    Data eligibility: rates ≤ 7 days old; forward P/E ≤ 14 days old; EPS proxy ≤ 10 days old with at least 30% SPY coverage and 10 companies; manual earnings fallback ≤ 14 days old. Missing or invalid required inputs mean UNAVAILABLE, never LOW. The combined regime remains UNDETERMINED while earnings are UNRATED.
